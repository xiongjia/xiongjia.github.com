## What this proves

A drag is not a `mousedown` handler plus `style.transform`. Motion's gesture layer handles pointer
capture, touch scrolling, momentum and constraints — and it updates `MotionValue`s that never enter
React state, which is why a 60 fps drag does not cause 60 renders.

## Decisions

- **`dragConstraints={boxRef}` instead of numbers.** Passing a ref measures the element and its
  container, so the drag area follows the layout — including a resized pane or a re-flowed card —
  without a resize listener.
- **`dragElastic` is exposed as a slider.** It is the one gesture parameter whose value is impossible
  to judge from documentation alone: 0 is a hard wall, 0.4 feels rubbery, 1 lets the card follow the
  pointer anywhere and spring back.
- **`dragSnapToOrigin` as a toggle.** "Pick up a card and put it back" is a real interaction, and the
  prop is a one-line version of it. Off, the card stays where it was dropped — the behaviour a
  free-form canvas wants.
- **The readout prints `MotionValue`s through `useSpring`.** The spring is what actually moves the
  card; printing the raw `x`/`y` would show a different number from the one on screen.
- **`touch-none` on the draggable.** Without it, a vertical drag on a phone scrolls the page instead
  of moving the card, and the gesture layer never sees the move.

## React's role

`onDrag` / `onDragEnd` are used only to update a small text readout. That is deliberate: a drag that
called `setState` per move would re-render the demo tree (and the editor above it, in a composed
app). Motion values are the state; React reads snapshots.

## Pitfalls

- `drag` is disabled here under reduced motion rather than shortened: a drag that snaps instantly is
  worse than a card that does not move, because the pointer and the card disagree.
- The constraint element must not be `overflow: hidden` in a way that clips the card's shadow — the
  demo keeps the clipping on the box and the shadow on the card, which is why the card is inset.
- `dragMomentum={false}` is the right setting for a "pick up and place" interaction; with momentum on,
  the card keeps sliding after release and the drop position is unpredictable.
- Gesture props do not compose with `layout` automatically: a draggable element inside a `layout`
  list needs the drag transform on an inner element, or the two animate the same transform.
