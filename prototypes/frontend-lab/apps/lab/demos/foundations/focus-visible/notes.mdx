## What this proves

`focus:` and `focus-visible:` are not interchangeable. Clicking a `focus:ring` button lights it up;
clicking a `focus-visible:ring` button does not. Tabbing lights up both.

## Decisions

- **`outline-none` is always paired with a replacement ring.** The first button shows what happens
  without one: the control becomes invisible to keyboard users. It is in the demo on purpose.
- **`focus-visible` is the default in this lab.** Browsers decide keyboard intent well enough now,
  and the alternative (`:focus`) makes pointer interactions noisy on every click.
- **`ring-offset-2` on filled buttons.** A ring drawn on the button edge disappears when the button
  sits on a same-coloured surface; the offset gives it a gap.
- **The "last input" badge exists for verification.** Focus visuals cannot be asserted from a
  screenshot, but "did the browser report keyboard or pointer" can be read from the DOM, which is how
  this page is checked in the batch log.

## Pitfalls

- `overflow-hidden` on an ancestor clips the ring; look at the parent before blaming the utility
- A `disabled` button is not focusable, so `focus-visible` never fires — reflect the state elsewhere
- Components that wrap a native control must not swallow `onFocus`/`onBlur` when they forward refs
