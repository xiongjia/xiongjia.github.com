### facilitated

- **type**: word
- **date**: 2026-08-11
- **source**: 视频采访（YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mVWywZLFt6c）
- **status**: learning
- **tags**: [formal, verb]
- **发音**: /fəˈsɪlɪteɪtɪd/
- **含义**: 促进、使……变得容易（facilitate 的过去式/过去分词）
- **英义**: to make an action or process easier; to help (something) happen or progress more smoothly
- **记忆**: 词根 facil-（拉丁 facilis「容易」，源自 facere「做」）→ facility 便利、facile 轻易的
- **语境**: 指在办公室上班促进了与实习生及同级同事的交流
- **原句**: Being at the office has facilitated my meeting with other interns and other people who were at the same level of career as I did.
- **造句**: The new tool facilitates collaboration across remote teams.
- **同义/反义**: ease, promote / hinder, impede

### solar eclipse

- **type**: collocation
- **date**: 2026-08-12
- **source**: 新闻/视频（YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K8Dwy0u5pp8）
- **status**: learning
- **tags**: [noun]
- **发音**: /ˈsəʊlə ɪˈklɪps/
- **含义**: 日食；对应 lunar eclipse（月食）——天体搭配用 solar/lunar，不用 sun/moon，如 solar system（太阳系）
- **英义**: an event in which the moon passes between the sun and the earth and hides the sun either completely or partly
- **记忆**: solar 词根 sol-（拉丁 sol「太阳」）→ solar system、solstice 至日；eclipse 希腊语 ekleipsis「消失」（ek 出 + leipein 离开）→ 日食 = 太阳被「遮没/离开视线」
- **语境**: 记「solar 不是 sun」；同视频另一句原句也提到人们涌向西班牙观看日食
- **原句**: People flocking to Spain to view solar eclipse could spark more devastating wildfires.
- **易混**: lunar eclipse 月食；solar system 太阳系（不说 sun system）
- **造句**: A solar eclipse occurs when the moon passes between the sun and the Earth.
