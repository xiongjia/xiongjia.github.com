# English Scraps Archive — 2026-w35

## 卡片

### contrary

- **type**: word
- **date**: 2026-08-29
- **source**: 未知来源（原句：On the contrary, she got herself involved in dangerous activities.）
- **status**: learning
- **tags**: [adjective, formal]
- **发音**: /ˈkɒntrəri/（英）/ /ˈkɑːntreri/（美）
- **含义**: 相反的，对立的；（固定搭配 on the contrary）恰恰相反
- **英义**: opposite in nature, direction, or meaning; *on the contrary* is used to introduce a statement that contradicts what has just been said
- **记忆**: contra-（相对，对着）+ -ary（形容词后缀）→ 对着来的 → 相反的
- **语境**: 先否定对方说法，再用 on the contrary 引出相反的事实，语气正式；原文有拼写错误（contray → contrary, activites → activities），已按正确拼写记录
- **原句**: On the contrary, she got herself involved in dangerous activities.
- **造句**: I didn't find the book boring; on the contrary, I couldn't put it down.
- **同义/反义**: opposite, opposed / same, in agreement

### accusations

- **type**: word
- **date**: 2026-08-29
- **source**: 未知来源（原句：I insist that the defendant is innocent and your accusations against him are not true.）
- **status**: learning
- **tags**: [noun, formal]
- **发音**: /ˌækjuˈzeɪʃn/（名词 accusation / accusations 同音）
- **含义**: 指控，控告；指责（复数 accusations 常用于指责多项行为）
- **英义**: a claim or statement that someone has done something wrong or illegal, especially one that has not yet been proven
- **记忆**: accuse（ac- 加强）+ cusare（控告，与 cause「诉讼」同源）+ -ation → 指控这件事
- **语境**: 常用复数；固定搭配 accusations against sb「对某人的指控」；动词 accuse sb of (doing) sth；原句强调指控「不属实」（not true），与 innocent 呼应——指控 ≠ 已证实
- **原句**: I insist that the defendant is innocent and your accusations against him are not true.
- **造句**: She denied all accusations of cheating in the exam.
- **同义/反义**: charge, allegation / defense, vindication
