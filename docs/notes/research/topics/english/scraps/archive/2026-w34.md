### avalanche

- **type**: word
- **date**: 2026-08-17
- **source**: 数据可视化教程（https://handsondataviz.org/choose.html）
- **status**: new
- **tags**: [noun]
- **发音**: /ˈævəlɑːnʃ/
- **含义**: 雪崩；（比喻）大量涌来的事物
- **英义**: a mass of snow, ice, and rocks falling rapidly down a mountainside; a sudden arrival or occurrence of something in overwhelming quantities
- **记忆**: 法语 avalanche，源自罗曼什语 lavina，来自拉丁 labina「滑坡」（labi「滑落」）
- **语境**: 用雪崩比喻蜂拥而至的数字工具——an avalanche of digital tools
- **原句**: If you feel overwhelmed by the avalanche of digital tools available today
- **造句**: The company received an avalanche of complaints after the price hike.
- **同义/反义**: deluge, flood / trickle

### rotated

- **type**: word
- **date**: 2026-08-19
- **source**: 未知来源（自记：API 凭证轮换）
- **status**: new
- **tags**: [technical, verb]
- **发音**: /rəʊˈteɪtɪd/
- **含义**: 轮换；定期更换（凭证/密钥/密码）
- **英义**: (v) to turn or cause to turn around an axis or center; (in security/IT) to regularly replace credentials, keys, or secrets with new ones as a security practice
- **记忆**: 拉丁 rota「轮子」→ rotate → 凭证像轮子一样定期转一圈换成新的；rotated 比 updated 更准确（updated 只强调更新，rotated 强调定期轮换的安全实践）
- **语境**: API 密钥定期轮换，避免长期使用同一密钥的安全风险；以前习惯用 updated，现在知道 rotated 更地道
- **原句**: API credentials have been rotated.
- **造句**: We rotate our database passwords every 90 days.
- **同义/反义**: cycle, refresh / keep, retain
